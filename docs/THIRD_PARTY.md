# Third-party icons

Generic picker icons use the AndroidX Material icons library under [Apache License 2.0](../licenses/Apache-2.0.txt). The Gradle wrapper uses the same license. The [Material icon assets](https://github.com/google/material-design-icons) originate with Google.

Kairo's launcher and notification marks are original. Before adding a service logo, document the official asset source, permission for use and redistribution, applicable brand guidelines, and any required attribution here. Use a generic icon when these terms are unclear. Older backups can still be imported. Custom images are ignored.

The searchable payment catalog uses Google's **Material Symbols Outlined** under Apache 2.0, adapted from Nia's pinned subset of [google/material-design-icons](https://github.com/google/material-design-icons/tree/10ecc14790612ca8fd164e3f4ffe9c16fc50d5b7). The vectors retain their source and modification notices, and the license is bundled in the APK.

## Service icons in 0.5

Proton, Proton Mail, Proton Drive, Proton VPN and Proton Calendar marks identify user-entered services only. Kairo is not affiliated with Proton. The official [Proton media kit](https://proton.me/media/kit) states that its brand assets may be freely used provided the accompanying media refers back to proton.me. Settings includes a visible Proton credit and links to proton.me and the media kit, and the service presets include the relevant Proton website.

The bundled flat renditions come from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/e451c581fccb01b56f47e1a77292bdd1fd95b1a4), distributed under CC0 1.0. Paths are converted to Android vectors with their catalog colors, without changing geometry. [service-icons.json](service-icons.json) records the pinned rendition URLs, official source and colors. The CC0 text is included in the APK at assets/licenses/Simple-Icons-CC0.txt. Copyright licensing does not grant ownership of the Proton trademarks. Do not use these marks as Kairo's branding or imply endorsement.

The other service presets use existing Material Symbols. They do not bundle those services' logos. A new logo requires a documented reuse basis and source before it is added.
